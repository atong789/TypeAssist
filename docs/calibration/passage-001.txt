the city pigeons hop by the empty porch at midnight, hardly minding the boy who hums a tiny tune. his hobby is to photograph the hilly horizon, hoping the morning light might bring out the pink ploughed fields beyond. i think the misty bay holds my history, the piers, the buoys, the high ivory homes. you might pity him, but he simply enjoys it. by noon the busy market booms with honey, ripe berries, and minty bread. nobody hurries him. he tips his hat, mounts his bicycle, and rides home, his mind quiet, his body tired but oddly happy today.
